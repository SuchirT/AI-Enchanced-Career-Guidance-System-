Try the live demo → See how it works
The problem

Career guidance today is generic — and confusion is the default.

Most students choose a career path based on peer pressure, incomplete information, or a one-size-fits-all counselling session — not on their own skills, interests and academic strengths.

01

One-size-fits-all advice

Generic counselling doesn't account for an individual's actual skills, interests or academic performance.

02

No visibility into skill gaps

Students rarely know which specific skills stand between them and the career they want.

03

Advice that doesn't evolve

Recommendations are given once and never update as the student's skills and progress change.

The solution

An AI-powered pathway, built around one student at a time.

Waypoint analyses a student's interests, skills, goals and academic performance to recommend suitable career pathways — then shows the exact roadmap to close the gap.

01

User profile

Academics, interests, skills and goals, captured once.

02

Skill assessment

Follow-up questions measure actual experience and depth.

03

Interest profiling

Interests are separated from existing technical abilities.

04

Career matching

Skills, interests and education are combined to rank pathways.

05

Adaptive roadmap

Existing skills are skipped and progress continuously changes the plan.
